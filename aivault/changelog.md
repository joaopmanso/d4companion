# Changelog

One line per change, newest first. Format: `YYYY-MM-DD · area · what changed`.

- 2026-10-30 · ocr · The rarity line read from the tooltip beats the catalog's (Hand of Apotheosis is no longer called mythic). The italic flavour quote under a power is skipped, a power's short last line ("potent.") is kept, and `100%[+]` markers (read `]+]`) and roll ranges are cut from the power. A unique's own affix pool no longer cuts "Fire Damage Multiplier" down to "Fire Damage".
- 2026-10-30 · ocr · Legendary and rare names are cleaned (`cleanName`): ®/©/ø inside a word is read as o, one- and two-character junk from icons and art is dropped, words snap onto known name words (aspect prefixes, item types, fixed names) and anything after the item type is cut. "Sadistic Excepti®Nal 5 Y J Boots" now reads "Sadistic Exceptional Boots".
- 2026-10-31 · design · The Diablo dress is back over the Hallmark layout (owner found the plain version empty and bland): `ornament.css` adds framed panels with gilt brackets, the fire wash, rarity glows, ember selection and gold meters; palette warmed, display face Cinzel; footer notice spans the full width.
- 2026-10-31 · design · Ancestral tiles keep their rarity edge; the white glow sits outside it, so a pasted Ancestral mythic stays purple.
- 2026-10-30 · ocr · Enigma (a runeword) is identified: when Tesseract reads the title after the type and Item Power lines, those lines are tried as the name too, and a tracked capital M read as `ITI`/`IVI` is fixed. Everything from `Properties lost when equipped` on (the equipped item's stats) is ignored instead of becoming sockets and power text. A type line wrapped after a slot word (`Unique Chest` / `Armor`) joins up, the rune list (`JahIthBer`) is no longer an affix, and `Unique Equipped`, `Seasonal Item` and `Lord of Hatred Item` are noise.
- 2026-10-30 · design · Full redesign with the Hallmark design skill (custom direction, "colour is loot"): one token system in `src/css/` replaces `styles.css` and `app.css`; Marcellus, Alegreya Sans and IBM Plex Mono for figures; iron-and-bone chrome where the only hues are rarity, verdicts and one blood red; a compact top bar over one tab row; no tracked capitals, gold brackets, glows or noise. Every screen recomposed inside the same wiring.
- 2026-10-30 · ocr · Local (adaptive) threshold instead of a global cut, so bright headers no longer hide the name and type line. Art junk beside the name is cut at the wide gap. The name pool is narrowed by rarity as well as slot ("FLEGY" → Elegy), and a unique can be identified by its power text. Weapon base lines are skipped and socket effects recorded. **Greater Affix, enchant and temper are read from each affix line's icon.** Fixes Elegy being read as a helm.
- 2026-10-30 · ocr · Items are identified by name from the new all-class `public/data/items.json` (`npm run items`, also rebuilt on every sync); rarity, type and slot come from the catalog. This fixes mythics being read as helms. Wrapped type lines, `Mythic Charm`, base stats ("288 All Resist") and the Quality line are handled, and unique powers are no longer read as affixes. Item check says when it can't tell the slot instead of assuming Helm.
- 2026-10-30 · ocr · Charms and seals: the reader recognises `Set Charm`/`Unique Charm`/`Seal` type lines, matches names past the art's junk, and reads multi-line Lucky Hit affixes. Item check shows a talisman verdict and "Put in talisman" instead of treating them as a helm. Footer text is centred.
- 2026-10-30 · process · Changes are committed straight to `main` (no PRs); each push deploys.
